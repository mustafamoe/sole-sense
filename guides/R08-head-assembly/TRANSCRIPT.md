# SoleSense R08 head assembly

Narration: synthetic macOS Samantha voice. No music.

## 00:00 - Assemble the R08 head

This guide assembles your R eight printed head. Match parts by shape and number; the colours are only for identification. Keep the video or PDF beside your bench, and pause after each step.

## 00:15 - Lay out the printed parts

Lay out the fourteen prints. The open chassis is forty two; the long moving carriage is forty four. The thin rectangular spring is cassette fifty seven. Inspect its beams for cracks, separated layers or bowing. Coupon fifty three is a fit test. Bridge fifty five is a temporary tool.

## 00:37 - Count the hardware first

Count the purchased hardware before starting. You need seven M two nuts and fourteen M three nuts, including three for the bridge. The four short adaptor screws must have button heads. Also collect the real magnet, Hall sensor and capacitor. Use small hand tools, and tighten gently.

## 00:59 - Prepare the chassis

Turn the chassis over. Fit four M three adaptor nuts, two M three sensor nuts, and two M two cassette nuts. A tiny adhesive tack can hold the deep cassette nuts; let it cure. Join adaptor forty one with four M three by eight button-head screws.

## 01:20 - Build the carriage on the bench

Work on carriage forty four outside the housing. Insert two M two nuts in the mounting posts and two in the magnet-retainer slots. Bond the six by three millimetre magnet with a thin adhesive layer and let it cure. Then fit retainer forty six with two M two by ten screws.

## 01:43 - Attach the moving rail from below

Place the wide moving rail against the bottoms of the two carriage posts. Drive two M two by eight screws upward from underneath the cassette. Their heads stay underneath. Complete this outside the housing, because the chassis floor blocks access later. Leave the narrow fixed rail free.

## 02:05 - Fit the small cartridge lock

Seat the remaining M two nut in the receiver side slot, then fit the M two by twelve cartridge lock. Knob fifty one is optional. Leave this small lock loose so the cartridge path stays clear.

## 02:20 - Lower the complete subassembly

Lower the carriage and cassette together into the open chassis. Hold the solid parts, never the thin beams. Rest the narrow fixed rail on its two pads, and place the large shoulder between the lower stops. The rail holes should align without bending the spring.

## 02:38 - Locate the carriage with bridge 55

Fit three M three nuts in bridge fifty five. Secure the bridge using the two front M three by thirty five cover screws, inserted from below. Add the M three by thirty service screw. It should enter the shoulder bore easily and hold the carriage near neutral.

## 02:58 - Secure the fixed rail from above

Now insert the other two M two by eight screws downward from above through the narrow fixed rail. Tighten evenly with the cassette flat. Remove the entire service screw and check gentle movement and free return. Stop if anything bows, rubs or stays displaced.

## 03:16 - Fit the stationary Hall sensor

Solder and insulate the Hall sensor leads and decoupling capacitor before seating the package. Its flat face points toward the magnet. Secure mount forty seven with two M three by six screws, centred in the slots. Route and strain-relieve wires along fixed structure and out through the side port.

## 03:39 - Exchange the bridge for the cover

Remove the service screw and both bridge mounting screws. Set the bridge aside with its three nuts. Fit five M three nuts into cover forty three, including the service-screw nut. Check the wires, lower the cover, and fasten all four M three by thirty five screws from underneath.

## 04:01 - Fit the rigid bench cartridge first

For the first bench test, use rigid cartridge forty nine. With the tip unloaded, install the service screw. Back the small cartridge lock out at least three millimetres. Align the lugs, insert the cartridge, turn thirty degrees, seat the flange and gently tighten the small lock.

## 04:21 - Remove the service screw. Test return.

Remove the entire long service screw, not just its knob. Leave the small cartridge lock tightened. Check gentle axial movement and free return without forcing the stops. Then wire the external bench electronics and calibrate against a checked balance. The printed spring has no established force rating until tested.
