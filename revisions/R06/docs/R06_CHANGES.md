# R06 — slimmer housing

The pressure head was wider than the handle/collar. R06 repackages its internals to reduce this visual and physical bulk.

| Dimension | R05 | R06 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Main housing width | 70 mm | 56 mm |
| Main housing length | 73 mm | 73 mm |
| Main housing height including lid | 38 mm | 38 mm |
| Front corner radius in plan | Square | 10 mm |
| Grip diameter | 46 mm | 46 mm |
| Twist-lock collar diameter | 62 mm | 62 mm |
| Tip centerline | Y=0 | Y=0 |
| Overall length with reference nylon | 283.4 mm | 283.4 mm |

![Same-scale comparison](../previews/04_Before_After.png)

The spring stage, magnet, Hall sensor and their fasteners move 7 mm sideways inside the head. The output cartridge remains centered on the handle. The leaves keep their 28 × 12 × 0.10 mm stock, 18 mm free span and 27 mm axial station spacing. Both travel limits and the nominal sensor gap remain the same. The offset output changes the load path; physical calibration and free-return testing are required for this assembly.

Reprint **41 adaptor, 42 chassis, 43 lid, 44 carriage and 55 assembly bridge** as a matched set. Existing R05 clamps, magnet cap, Hall mount, cartridges, knobs and fit coupons can be reused after fit inspection; their local part geometry is unchanged. Handle, vibration head and mechanical joint geometry remain unchanged. Purchased-part quantities and lengths are unchanged.

The head is 10 mm wider than the grip but 6 mm narrower than the collar. Making it flush with the 46 mm grip would require a further mechanism redesign. This revision prioritizes the existing spring geometry and access for the first bench build.

The package contains CAD, individual STLs, six geometry-only 3MF plates and refreshed assembly views. Geometric checks do not establish printer fit, mechanical strength, measurement accuracy or clinical readiness.
