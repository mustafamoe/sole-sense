# SoleSense R04 animated walkthrough

[Play or download the video](SoleSense_R04_Showcase.mp4)

A 44-second, silent, captioned video rendered from the R04 CAD meshes at 1280 × 720 and 24 frames per second. The camera moves around the device and the parts follow the proposed exchange motions.

| Time | What is shown |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | Pressure configuration and separate vibration head, rotating overview |
| 0:06–0:18 | Safety slider, collar unlock, pressure-head removal, vibration-head insertion, relocking |
| 0:18–0:22 | Rounded vibration tip with schematic wave graphics |
| 0:22–0:25 | Larger 1.3-inch display enclosure and illustrative screen |
| 0:25–0:33 | Cartridge unlock, withdrawal, prepared replacement and relocking |
| 0:33–0:39 | Manual movement and schematic filament flex against a neutral test surface |
| 0:39–0:44 | Overview from the opposite side |

The head stays keyed while the collar rotates. Both collar halves rotate together; their assembly bolts are not removed during a head exchange. The cartridge hub and filament are changed together. The video shows the pressure head already reattached when it cuts to the screen and cartridge sections.

This is a design explanation, not a recording of a working device. Spring contacts are provisional, the display is a mockup, and the vibration waves and filament bow are illustrations rather than physical simulation. Force, electrical operation, retention, patient-contact materials and hygiene require physical development and testing. No clinical force or vibration frequency is represented.

The editable animation is [SoleSense_R04_Animation.blend](../cad/SoleSense_R04_Animation.blend). The original CAD solids are unchanged. Animation checks are recorded in [animation_validation.json](animation_validation.json).
