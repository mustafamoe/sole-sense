SOLESENSE R05 — FIRST-FIT BENCH PROTOTYPE
20 October 2026

Print plate 01 first and test purchased hardware in the coupons.
Use millimetres and 100% scale; do not scale the assembly to fix tight holes.
Starting assumption: FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, PLA.
Housing: 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, 35–40% infill.
Carriage, spring clamps, cartridges and knobs: solid infill.
Plates 02/03 contain the new head. Plates 04–06 are retained handle/vibration parts.
The 3MF files contain geometry only. Select the actual printer/material and review supports per object.
The nose-down carriage needs supports, including supports starting on the model under shoulders.
Remove supports without damaging spring seating faces, nut pockets or bayonet tracks.
Do not print the metal leaf-spring references. Do not print an assembled STEP as one part.
Read BUILD_GUIDE.md for the numbered assembly order and hardware list.
These files have CAD/mesh checks but no physical print or force calibration.
