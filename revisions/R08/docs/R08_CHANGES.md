# R07 → R08

| Component | R08 change |
|---|---|
| Two phosphor-bronze leaves | Removed; replaced by one printed two-beam cassette |
| Four separate leaf clamps 45 | Removed |
| Eight M2 × 10 leaf screws and eight nuts | Replaced by four M2 × 8 screws and four nuts |
| Chassis 42 | Two low fixed-rail pads and underside nut pockets replace tall leaf seats |
| Carriage 44 | Two low posts with side-loaded nuts replace tall moving leaf seats |
| Bridge 55 | Tool-access notch for the front fixed-rail screw |
| Baseline cassette 57 | 0.8 mm beams, 24 mm root-line span, 2.4 mm height, 0.6 mm root fillets |
| Alternative cassettes 58/59 | 1.0 and 1.2 mm beams; same mounting pattern |
| Travel stops | 2.0 mm compression and 0.6 mm extension, reduced from 2.4/1.0 |
| Leaf length template 54 | Removed |
| Housing exterior, cover, adaptor, inlay | Same sculpted form and dimensions |
| Magnet, Hall mount, cartridge, handle, display and vibration geometry | Retained |

The baseline cassette is 43 × 35.4 × 2.4 mm. All three cassettes are complete alternatives; only one is fitted.

Real metal still remains in the magnet, electrical conductors and fasteners. These components were deliberately retained. The mechanism is easier to source and print, but plastic spring drift and repeatability introduce new validation work.

R07 is preserved. Its video and build package show metal leaves. R08 has new CAD-derived views and its own matched print package.

This revision does not make the earlier buying spreadsheet fully compatible. Sensor choice, magnet size, display and board mounts must be reconciled with the actual purchased parts before a complete electronics build.
