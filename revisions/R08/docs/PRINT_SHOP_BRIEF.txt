SoleSense R08 — experimental bench prototype

Units: mm. Scale: 100%. No machine-specific G-code supplied.
Starting assumption: FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer, PLA.
Confirm actual printer, material, build volume and toolpaths before printing.

PRINT FIRST:
52 hardware coupon; 53 receiver coupon; 48 cartridge.
57 baseline printed flexure (0.8 mm beams).
58 and 59 are optional complete replacement cassettes, NOT additional installed parts.

CASSETTES:
43 x 35.4 x 2.4 mm, supplied flat. No supports. Solid fill.
Inspect continuous beam paths, roots and holes in every layer.
Do not allow the slicer to omit thin walls or leave unbonded lines.
Do not attach a brim to the narrow beams or remove material from them.
Inspect for cracks, gaps, warped rails and damaged roots.

MAIN HEAD:
Use R08 chassis 42 and carriage 44 with R08 cassette and bridge 55.
Keep support residue off mounting faces and tracks.
Chassis prints open upward.
Lid crown upward; support underneath.
Carriage supplied nose-down; review shoulders/pockets and accessible support.
White shell and teal cassette/inlay are visual preferences only.

3MF plates are neutral geometry on a 180 x 180 mm virtual bed.
They contain no printer/material/support settings. Rearrange if needed.
Do not print the assembled STEP or reference meshes as one object.
Real magnet, Hall sensor, electronics and fasteners are still required.
No measured force accuracy or printed-spring service life is established.
Read BUILD_GUIDE.md and SHOPPING_LIST.md before assembly.
