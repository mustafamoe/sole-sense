# SoleSense R03 — labeled component views

Eight 3200 × 2200 render sheets identify every modeled device component. Open the PNG images at full size to read the fine component notes.

- **P01–P31**: printed parts. Numbers match STL filename prefixes. Six numbers are fit coupons, listed in the index rather than installed in the assembly.
- **H01–H20**: the 20 nominal hardware references in the mechanical design.
- **H21–H25**: head retaining screws, separated connector halves and illustrative wire bundles.
- **F01–F03**: integral openings/alignment features, not separate components.

`COMPONENT_KEY.md`, `.csv` and `.json` contain the complete index. The 25 installed printed parts and all 20 hardware references appear across the exploded sheets. Most small fasteners, inserts and assembly consumables are not modeled individually; see `assembly_and_printing.md` for those quantities.

`SoleSense_R03_Labeled_Views.blend` contains eight named scenes. Each has a separate `LABELS` collection with editable text, badges and leader lines. Use the scene selector to change views. The active scene opens on the exploded handle. The labels are arranged for the saved camera views.

Some stacked parts are separated farther in these illustrations so the labels can point to visible surfaces. Exploded distances are presentation offsets, not assembly dimensions. Hardware blocks are simplified envelopes, not detailed supplier CAD. The original STEP, STL and engineering files are unchanged.

The original assembly and wiring notes are included for reference. This remains the same R03 engineering prototype; labeling does not establish physical fit, force calibration or electrical performance.
