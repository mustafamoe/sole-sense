# SoleSense R03 — additional views

Eight additional renders from the existing R03 mechanical model. Dimensions and print geometry are unchanged.

- 05: opposite side, filament head attached and vibration head alongside.
- 06: top orthographic view.
- 07: underside and charging access.
- 08: exploded handle with its electronics trays, battery, display and shell.
- 09: exploded filament head, carrier, nylon inserts, probe and sensor.
- 10: exploded vibration head, driver, motor and dome cap.
- 11: separated head connection, keyed pilot, retaining screws and service lead.
- 12: vibration head assembled on the shared handle.

Green/blue electronics and black connector blocks are simplified component envelopes. The lead shapes in view 11 illustrate the manual disconnect and service loop. Exploded distances are presentation offsets, not assembly dimensions.

Open `SoleSense_R03_Expanded_Views.blend` to explore the eight named scenes. Original CAD, print files and full assembly instructions remain in the R03 engineering package.
